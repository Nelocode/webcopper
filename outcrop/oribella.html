The Oribella project comprises 20,000 hectares located in the Antioquia Department within the Middle Cauca Belt, a metallogenic province hosting major gold-copper porphyries.

Overview

Oribella is located within the Middle Cauca Belt province, which is highly prospective and hosts major gold-copper porphyries such as Buriticá and Nuevo Chaquiro (Quebradona). Outcrop recently expanded Oribella with new mineral applications and is currently looking for a joint venture partner.

Accessibility

The project is easily accessible from Medellín by paved roads to the Concordia municipality. This infrastructure simplifies logistics, field work, and technical deployment in the region.

Geological mapping and geochemistry indicate the presence of structurally controlled mineralization and blind porphyry systems.

Lithology

Oceanic Domains

Lithology includes oceanic domains of deep-water pelagic and turbidite-laminated sediments, submarine tuffs, and basaltic volcanic rocks.

Geochemistry

Soil Anomalies

Robust gold and copper soil anomalies (500 m by 250 m) are open in two directions, showing epithermal zonation and near-surface gold mineralization.

Trenching

1.1 g/t Gold Subsurface

Hand trenching within the soil anomaly shows 11 m @ 1.1 g Au/t in the subsurface, highlighting high-grade potential near surface.
