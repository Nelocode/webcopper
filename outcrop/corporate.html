Corporate

Overview, Leadership & Governance

Outcrop Silver is a Canadian company actively exploring for precious metals in Colombia. With corporate offices in Vancouver, Canada and operational offices in Medellin, Colombia, we are focused on identifying and expanding high-grade mineral deposits.

Operational Focus

Outcrop Silver is rapidly advancing the Santa Ana high-grade silver deposit with ongoing expansion and exploration drilling. Exploration work is conducted by a highly disciplined professional team with decades of experience in Colombia.

Our primary listing is on the Toronto Stock Exchange (TSX) under the symbol OCG. In addition, our shares are traded in the United States (OTCQX: OCGSF) and in Germany (FSE: MRG).

Key Strengths

  • Experienced Management
  • High Discovery Rate Areas
  • Drill-Ready Outcrops
  • Strong Local Communities Support

Our team brings together decades of global exploration, mining operations, corporate finance, and community relations expertise, with a proven track record in Colombia.
