Mallama Project

High-Grade Epithermal Gold-Silver System

Nariño
Location (Colombia)
100%
Ownership

Primary Minerals
Advanced
Exploration Stage

The Mallama project comprises 9,036 hectares of mineral titles located in the Piedrancha district of Nariño, Colombia. It hosts more than 35 mapped epithermal veins with extensive historical production.

Overview

Located approximately 126 km by road from San Juan de Pasto, the property covers a large portion of the Piedrancha district. Over 35 mapped and projected epithermal veins have strike lengths of up to 5 km. Piedrancha is historically recognized as one of the largest and most important high-grade epithermal districts in Colombia.

History

Gold and silver production from artisanal mining started in post-colonial times during the 19th century. Today, at least eight artisanal mines are active on the property, producing high-grade gold and silver from the Bombona zone. Extensive exploration was conducted in the 1980s by the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA). Recent sampling conducted by Outcrop in the Bombona zone shows gold grades exceeding 15 g Au/t.

Mallama is situated within a well-endowed tectonic and mineral belt hosting major metal discoveries in South America.

Regional Belt

Middle Cauca Extension

Mallama lies within the mineralized corridor hosting Cascabel in Ecuador and La Colosa in Colombia, two world-class porphyry and epithermal deposit discoveries.

Vein System

High-Grade Veins

Prevalence of high-angle, high-grade gold-silver epithermal veins averaging 1.0 m in width, with strike lengths extending up to 5 km across the district.

Sampling Grades

23.3 g/t Gold Average

Weighted average mining grades in the largest accessible working of the Bombona zone show 23.3 g Au/t and 182 g Ag/t, confirming high-grade mineralization.

Outcrop Silver is committed to responsible exploration, working closely with indigenous communities and seeking structural partners.

Community Relations

Mallama includes lands recognized as traditional homelands of indigenous peoples, requiring a consultation process called Consulta Previa. Outcrop has initiated baseline studies to build respectful, effective relationships. Additionally, the company aims to incorporate local informal/artisanal miners into future modern exploration and mining activities.

Future Plans

The company is currently focusing on a 1,200-hectare portion of the project to complete an environmental study to subtract the titles from the regional Forestry Reserve. Outcrop is actively seeking a funding joint venture partner to accelerate exploration across the wider district.

Outcrop Silver is a mineral exploration company focused on locating and developing high-grade silver deposits in Colombia.
