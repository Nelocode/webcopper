Argelia Project

High-Grade Gold-Silver Epithermal Target

The Argelia project comprises 4,355 hectares of mineral applications located in the Antioquia Department. It features intermediate sulfidation epithermal veins and a rich mining tradition.

Overview

Located approximately 75 kilometers southeast of Medellín, Argelia lies in a region of significant epithermal and porphyry potential. Sonsón batholith hosts high-grade gold and silver mineralization. The project is accessible by paved and gravel roads.

History

Argelia has a long-standing mining history. Public records show that a private British mining company successfully mined and produced gold on the project prior to 1950. The historical underground workings feature two main mining levels spaced approximately 100 meters apart in elevation.

Intermediate sulfidation veins are hosted in the Sonsón batholith within a regional structural shear corridor.

Lithology

Sonsón Batholith

Host rocks consist of the intrusive Sonsón batholith that cut metamorphic rocks of the regional Cajamarca Complex.

Structure

4 km Shear Zone

Veins strike oblique to a shear zone (4 km long, 2 km wide) containing a fault corridor with three sub-parallel fault-veins (0.25 to 2.0 m widths).

Exploration

Artisanal Workings Visited

Reconnaissance in 2013 visited six active and abandoned artisanal mines (Diamond, Molino, Alirio, Santa Inés, Tesoro, Salón), confirming high-grade mineralization.

Mapping, geochemical grids, and local stakeholder consultation represent key milestones before future drilling campaigns.

Exploration Work

Prior to drilling, Outcrop plans to complete detailed mapping and geochemistry to define the extensions of structural corridors, vein geometries, and ore shoot controls in the primary target areas.

Social Licensing & Partnering

Outcrop intends to expand social and communication work with local community leaders to secure titles. The company is actively seeking a joint venture partner to finance exploration in Argelia.

Outcrop Silver is a mineral exploration company focused on locating and developing high-grade silver deposits in Colombia.
