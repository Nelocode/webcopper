Santa Ana Advantages

Santa Ana is a true primary silver project with strategic advantages that are expected to facilitate faster permitting and development than is possible for most mining projects

TRUE PRIMARY SILVER PROJECT

With 73% of indicated AgEq resources being from silver, this is a true primary silver project with great scarcity value.

HIGH GRADE RESOURCES

Allows a smaller mining operation and plant to produce substantial quantities of silver and gold.

VEINS AT SURFACE

Mining can be sequenced so that higher grade zones are targeted early in the mine plan. Newly discovered high-grade veins can be incorporated into mine plans relatively easily.

EXCELLENT INFRASTRUCTURE

Road access with power, water, labor, and services available should reduce capital and operating costs, as well as accelerate time to production.

SIMPLE METALLURGY

Initial testing suggests high silver and gold recoveries using simple gravity and flotation methods, without the need for cyanide, resulting in a high value precious metals concentrate.

STREAMLINED PERMITTING

The small mining footprint resulting from selective underground mining and a small processing plant, along with strong community and local/regional government support is expected to streamline the permitting process for Santa Ana.
