Santa Ana Project

Santa Ana covers over 36,000 hectares in Tolima, consolidating the historic Mariquita Silver District—the highest-grade primary silver district in Colombia. With a Maiden Resource Estimate declaring significant indicated and inferred resources, it represents our primary development asset.

Location Falan, Tolima, Colombia
Ownership 100% Owned
Primary Minerals Silver - Gold (Ag-Au)
Resource Estimate 24.2 Moz AgEq Indicated & Inferred

Mallama Project

Located in Nariño, Mallama covers 9,036 hectares of the Piedrancha epithermal vein district. It hosts more than 35 mapped veins with active artisanal mining on high-grade veins (up to 23.3 g Au/t).

Location Piedrancha District, Nariño
Ownership 100% Owned
Stage Advanced Exploration (Consulta Previa)

Oribella Project

Oribella comprises 20,000 hectares in the prospective Middle Cauca Belt. Geochemical soil anomalies and trenching suggest the presence of a blind gold-copper porphyry system.

Location Betulia & Concordia, Antioquia
Ownership 100% Owned
Stage Early Prospecting (Partner Seeking)

Argelia Project

Argelia covers 4,355 hectares of mineral applications. Hosts intermediate sulfidation veins within a 2.5 km fault corridor. Features pre-1950 British company historical workings.

Location Argelia, Antioquia
Ownership 100% Owned (Applications)
Stage Reconnaissance & Mapping
