Santa Ana Project

Our flagship high-grade primary silver district in Tolima

The Santa Ana Project comprises over 36,000 hectares located in the department of Tolima, Colombia, 190 km west of Bogota. It covers the historic Mariquita Silver District, the highest-grade silver district in Colombia with mining records dating back to 1585.

Historic Significance

Mariquita was the most important colonial silver production area in the New Kingdom of Granada (now Colombia). Historical reports indicate that operations achieved exceptional grades, sometimes exceeding thousands of grams of silver per tonne from narrow veins.

Outcrop Silver is the first modern exploration company to consolidate this historic district. We control 100% of the project and have identified multiple high-grade shoot systems that remain open for exploration.

Mineralization at Santa Ana is hosted within low-to-intermediate sulfidation epithermal quartz veins that cut metamorphic rocks of the Cajamarca formation.

District Scale

30+ km Strike Length

Quartz veins cut the metamorphic rocks of the regional Cajamarca formation, with parallel vein systems mapped across 30 kilometers. Only a small fraction has been drill-tested to date.

Vein Geometry

0.5m to 4m Widths

High-grade silver-gold mineralization is concentrated in steeply dipping quartz vein systems. Exceptionally high mineral concentration is found within defined localized shoots.

Metallurgy

No Refractory Treatment

Metallurgical testing proves outstanding recovery rates using standard flotation and gravity separation. Simple, conventional processing reduces capital costs.
